Privacy policy

Inbox Dodge

Last updated October 4, 2026 · Developer: Michael Donerkiel

In short: the game itself collects nothing about you. Your score stays on your phone. The game is free because it shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google collects some device and usage data to show and measure those ads.

What the game stores

Inbox Dodge saves your best score and your sound on/off setting on your device. This information never leaves your phone, and deleting the app deletes it.

There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no chat. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, or precise location.

Advertising

Inbox Dodge shows ads through Google AdMob, part of Google LLC. Ads appear between some rounds, and as an optional "watch an ad to keep going" choice after a game ends. To deliver and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device type, operating system, and app version
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown or tapped
  • Diagnostic data, such as crashes or performance problems in the ad software

Google's use of this data is covered by Google's own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Tracking. iOS asks whether Inbox Dodge may track you. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent. Where the law requires it, including the European Economic Area, the UK, and some US states, the app shows a consent message before any ads load. You can accept, refuse, or manage your choices there.
  • Ads in general. You can limit ad personalization across apps in your device settings.

Children

Inbox Dodge is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
